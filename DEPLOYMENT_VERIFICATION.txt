## 📝 Billing Calculation Example

```
Customer adds 3 items to cart:
  1. Veg Pahari Feast Momo × 1 = ₹169
  2. Pani Puri × 2 = ₹99 × 2 = ₹198
  3. Mango Lassi × 1 = ₹149

Subtotal = ₹169 + ₹198 + ₹149 = ₹516
Tax (5%) = ₹516 × 0.05 = ₹25.80
Service (10%) = ₹516 × 0.10 = ₹51.60
Total = ₹516 + ₹25.80 + ₹51.60 = ₹593.40

All calculations dynamic and real-time ✓
```
